Talk to Otis

Type or hold the mic and speak to talk to Otis…

Otis is named for James Otis Jr. and Mercy Otis Warren — a Massachusetts brother and sister who showed up for their fellow patriots at the start of the American Revolution. Ask Otis anything, and he'll point the way.

OUR MISSION

Help Mass lead in AI

Massachusetts has the talent, companies, and momentum to lead the AI transformation in our areas of strength — healthcare, education, robotics, life sciences, defense, and climate tech. Here's why builders are choosing to stay.

01

Unmatched talent density

The talent capital of the world: MIT, Harvard, founders, operators, PhDs, researchers, and more.

02

Access to capital

Deep access to capital across venture firms, universities, and angels.

03

Tight-knit founder community

Small enough to know everyone. Big enough to matter.

04

World-class healthcare

MGH, Brigham, Children's, Dana-Farber — the best on the planet.

05

Pro-innovation government

Leadership that wants you here, with the same goals to lead in AI.
