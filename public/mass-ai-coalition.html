The idea

Boston wayfinding meets championship banner

The coalition wants to make Massachusetts the global leader in AI, and its site has to speak to founders, students, investors and visitors all at once. I redesigned it around one question, "what brings you here?", so every visitor picks a path and gets a clear next step.

For the look, I borrowed from the city itself. A single red line runs down the page like the T's Red Line, drawn in as you scroll. Each section is a station, with its name set like a transit sign, and the page ends at a terminal stop: "End of the Line."

Built on the coalition's own brand

Colors and type come from the official brand guide, with every text color checked for contrast. Red owns whole moments (the Line, the main buttons, one fully red section) and the rest of the page stays night-dark.

Boston Black#0A0A0A

Coalition Red#CC0000

Deep Red#990000

Pure White#FFFFFF

Headlines use Barlow Condensed in heavy caps, body text uses DM Sans, and labels and numbers use Overpass, a typeface based on American highway signage. That keeps the clock, dates and stats reading as one wayfinding system.

This is a personal redesign concept, not an official Massachusetts AI Coalition project. The coalition's name, logo and brand belong to them; I designed the layout, motion and hero film, building them with Claude and Higgsfield.
